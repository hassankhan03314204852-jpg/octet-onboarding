Our 14 Departments

🔴
HR Department

Employee relations, onboarding & offboarding, payroll & bonus processing, scheduling, write-ups, promotions, and employee concerns.

🟢
Administrative Department

Office boys supervision, daily checklists verification, records maintenance, and weekly compliance reviews.

🟡
Recruitment Department

Job postings, CV shortlisting, interview scheduling, candidate communication, and end-to-end hiring process management.

🔴
Finance Department

Daily sales verification, Profit & Loss reports, payroll review, tax compliance, vendor/customer reconciliation, and bonus processing.

🟣
Surveillance Department

Camera-based store monitoring, penalty application, cash handling verification, EOD checks, and customer experience monitoring.

🟠
Projects & Maintenance

Two sub-departments: Heating & Cooling (HVAC, fridges, etc.) and General Maintenance (grease trap, fire system, grill, lights, etc.).

🔵
Training Department ★

New employee training, e-learning lesson creation & updates, feedback management, and training report submissions. Scroll down to learn more.

🟢
Inspection Department

Store audits, negative review handling, food quality checks, and Head Office report follow-ups.

🟡
Operations Department

Head Office coordination, P&L review, store operational issue resolution, and District Manager meetings.

🔴
Marketing Department

Social media, digital ads, graphic design, content writing, video editing, and website management.

🟣
Software Department

Technical support, bug testing and reporting, product research, and e-learning system issue resolution.

🟠
Rental Department

Room and property vacancy tracking, tenant dealing, lease signing, rent follow-up, and property management.

⚪
Compliance Department

Reporting checks across all departments, ticket management, error identification, and reporting to senior management.

🔵
Transvision Immigration

Canadian immigration services — PR, LMIA, work permit application preparation and client guidance.
